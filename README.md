A 100-word monologue for a dark lord who has burned libraries, raised the dead, and razed kingdoms before breakfast — and who now keeps postponing his own dawn assault, one small deferral at a time. The tiredness is never stated; it lives in the rhythm, the trailing sentences, and a throne that is mostly a chair.

`main.py` writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
